References combine cached catalog photos, the shop's own oil-canister photograph, existing published timing/shock component compositions, and local product references. These are generated editorial illustrations of automotive subjects, not replacements for SKU photos or proof of vehicle compatibility. Printed labels and promotional lettering were omitted; HTML titles and controls remain separate.

Full generated PNG originals are persisted under output/article-images/originals/ (local, ignored); a review sheet is output/article-images/contact-sheet.jpg. Built-in generated originals are retained in the Codex generated_images directory.

Each cover has 640/960/1200px 16:9 WebP variants and dedicated 4:3 and 1:1 crops for article structured data. Version filenames if regenerating because static assets use immutable caching.

The scoped manifest replaces legacy covers on the existing published articles. New Storage uploads and explicit image removal take precedence; originals remain available in the editor. Article copy and database records are unchanged.
